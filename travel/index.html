Journeys

2025.08 one journey

Beijing → Xi’an → Lanzhou → Xining → Qinghai Lake → Urumqi

Xi’an

Took the train from Beijing to Xi’an. The lasting impressions were Xi’an City Wall, the Terracotta Warriors, and the Qin bronze chariots and horses at the Emperor Qinshihuang’s Mausoleum Site Museum.

Lanzhou

Continued from Xi’an to Lanzhou. The Yellow River, Baita Mountain Park, and Zhongshan Bridge stood out. So did a particularly good cup of tianpeizi milk tea, made with sweet fermented grains.

Xining

Xining felt remarkably cool. I did not explore much of the city, but the beef offal I had for breakfast left a strong impression.

Qinghai Lake

Qinghai Lake was vast and spectacular.

On the train to Urumqi

Took the high-speed train to Urumqi. The views along the way were unforgettable: Shandan Horse Farm, the black Gobi, rows of wind turbines, and even a tornado in the distance.
