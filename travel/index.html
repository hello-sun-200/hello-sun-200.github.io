Journeys

2025.08

Xi’an → Lanzhou → Xining → Qinghai Lake → Urumqi

Xi’an

The lasting impressions were Xi’an City Wall, the Terracotta Warriors, and the Qin bronze chariots and horses at the Emperor Qinshihuang’s Mausoleum Site Museum.

Lanzhou

Continued from Xi’an to Lanzhou. The Yellow River, Baita Mountain Park, and Zhongshan Bridge stood out. So did a particularly good cup of tianpeizi milk tea, made with sweet fermented grains.

Xining

Xining felt remarkably cool. I visited Dongguan Grand Mosque, Xiguan Mosque, and a night market; the religious atmosphere left a strong impression. So did the beef offal I had for breakfast and a bowl of cooked highland barley.

Qinghai Lake

Qinghai Lake was vast and spectacular.

On the train to Urumqi

Took the high-speed train to Urumqi. The views along the way were unforgettable: Shandan Horse Farm, the black Gobi, rows of wind turbines, and even a tornado in the distance.

Urumqi

Around Urumqi, I visited the Tianshan Mountains, Red Hill Park, and the Xinjiang International Grand Bazaar. I also tried laohangua—a soft, sweet local melon—and dapanji, Xinjiang-style big plate chicken.

2026.02

Hong Kong → Macao

Hong Kong

Visited Victoria Harbour, Central, The University of Hong Kong, Stanley, and Tai Kwun, including the former Victoria Prison. I watched a lion dance, rode the Star Ferry, and took a sunset bus ride. The seaside character of southern Hong Kong Island left a lasting impression.

Macao

Visited several casinos. The Tree of Prosperity at Wynn Macau was especially striking. The Ruins of St. Paul’s and Mount Fortress also stood out.

Food

The strongest food memories were Demae Iccho noodles and toast with a soft-centred egg.
