Both strength sessions also begin with three-direction dumbbell lunges. Working sets are generally 3 × 6; face pulls are 3 × 12. Warm-up sets are separate, and the current loads allow every prescribed set to be completed reliably. Routine bicycle commuting adds at least 7.5 km on a weekday. A brief hanging stretch is done before leaving work each day.

The weekend loop

Weekend mornings start late and skip breakfast. Saturday's first meal is at about 10:00: one mackerel fillet with noodles, plus any small amount of beef left from the week. The afternoon hike carries its own carbohydrate supply. After returning, dinner is built around skin-on, bone-in chicken, tofu, and vegetables; some of the weekend noodles may also move to this meal.

Sunday's first meal is also at about 10:00, using the remaining chicken and tofu. The evening stays light. Across the weekend, vegetables are definite, with leafy greens and mushrooms preferred, and kiwifruit is the chosen whole fruit. The exact vegetable, fruit, and noodle allocations remain intentionally flexible.

The two-day working quantities are about 1 kg raw skin-on, bone-in chicken, 800 g tofu, and 300 g dry noodles, plus one Saturday mackerel fillet, vegetables, mushrooms, and an appropriate amount of kiwifruit.

This page is a record, not a prescription. Changes are made from observed weight, recovery, appetite, sleep, and training performance—not from the desire to make the plan look more sophisticated.
